**Lessons**
- `[workflow]` The run-14 method held: agreement between independent pickers predicts survival (29 of 32).
- `[workflow]` When more than the cap pass, rank with judges on different lenses and drop by sub-topic first: nine
  of the ten dropped were the weaker of two on one topic.
- `[scorer]` On a clean transcript with one dominant storyteller Sonnet is good: 78% of its picks passed.
  It still runs past the payoff and still scores 9s as 8s.
- `[scorer]` Sonnet misses the hosts' own lines when the guest is the star (266, 273).
- `[scorer]` Sonnet clipped a payoff that is a sexual joke about the guest's child and only flagged it. That is a
  reject, not a risk flag.
- `[universal]` TRACK's talker pick fails on a wide shot when the storyteller has a mic over his mouth and the
  others laugh: 17 of 20 clips sat on a listener. Look at a strip of every render, not one clip.
- `[universal]` Whisper tokens like "10 -year -old" and "$3 ,000" reach the captions as written; join them in code.
- `[theme]` One legendary storyteller guest: 30 moments at 8+ in 69 minutes (26/hour), 17/hour after the cap.
