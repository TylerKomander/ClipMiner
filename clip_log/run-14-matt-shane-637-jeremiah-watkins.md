**Lessons**
- `[workflow]` Tko starts the app, not GURT. A render inside an app GURT launched was killed mid-file.
- `[workflow]` A wait loop must be tested against the failed state before it is trusted.
- `[workflow]` Independent blind pickers plus an editor and a skeptic work as GURT's pass; agreement between
  pickers predicts survival. Merge candidates by payoff line, not by overlapping time.
- `[scorer]` Sonnet's precision dropped on a loose three-comedian episode (10 of 15) and every kept pick needed a
  new boundary; it still runs past the payoff.
- `[universal]` On a transcript missing its curse words, a dropped curse after "the" can be restored on the word
  after it, when that word appears once in the clip ("would" → "**** would").
- `[theme]` Three comedians, no topic: 14 clips/hour, same as run 9.
