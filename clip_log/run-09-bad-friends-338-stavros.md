**Lessons**
1. `[scorer]` Model rejects a payoff whose setup is minutes earlier, or clips the payoff alone. Splice setup + payoff.
2. `[scorer]` Model calls quick back-and-forth or one voice with "yeah"s "cross-talk" and rejects it.
3. `[scorer]` Code lost the model's finds after the model did its job (length after cuts, a short cut quote). Fix the
   handoff before blaming the judgment.
4. `[universal]` Poster faces at a third of the real face's size pass the size filter, but TRACK stays on the
   talker because posters don't talk. Look at frames anyway.
